KORA — PRODUCT PREVIEW

Kora is being designed to connect inventory, orders, payments and receipts across a merchant website, WhatsApp and the counter.

This preview uses sample data; no live bank account, stock database or customer messaging is connected.

Production controls to implement and test: tenant isolation, role permissions, signed provider events, server verification, atomic stock reservations, immutable movement history, backup recovery and delivery retries.

Hosting regions, subprocessors, retention and support arrangements must be confirmed before live merchant onboarding.
