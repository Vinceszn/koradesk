Questions owners ask

Is Kora a website builder, inventory system or WhatsApp assistant? +

The vision brings all three together. One commerce backend holds products, stock and orders; the website, WhatsApp and counter are different ways to sell. The first MVP focuses on inventory, orders, verified payments and receipts.

Can I use Kora without a physical shop? +

Yes. Online sellers need the same product, stock, order and payment records. The pilot is being designed for businesses selling countable physical products, whether online, in person or both.

Is the full platform available now? +

This site is a product preview. The animated orders, stock changes and payments are simulated. We are planning the founding pilot; the storefront, automatic WhatsApp delivery and voice features will be released in stages.

How does Kora know a customer has paid? +

The planned integration checks a connected payment provider’s transaction records against the order reference, amount and currency. An uploaded screenshot can help with matching, but does not establish that money arrived.

What happens when two people want the last item? +

The backend is designed to check and reserve stock in one database transaction. An unpaid hold can expire; a paid order keeps its reservation until fulfilment or cancellation is handled.

Can I connect my existing ecommerce store? +

Existing-store connectors are on the roadmap. We will start with a focused inventory and order core, then add a Kora storefront and selected integrations. No Shopify, WooCommerce or bank connection is active in this preview.

Will Kora answer phone calls? +

Voice ordering is a later feature. We plan to validate text ordering and stock accuracy first, followed by voice notes and a limited inbound calling pilot.
