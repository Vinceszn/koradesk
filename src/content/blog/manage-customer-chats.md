---
title: "Your chats and checkout need one order list"
description: "Keep the customer conversation connected to a saved order."
pubDate: 2026-10-15
minutes: 2
---

A conversation is where an order starts, not a dependable stock ledger. Save the customer, variant, quantity and agreed price in one order record.

When an assistant is added, it should use the same order backend as the website and staff dashboard. Unclear requests need a question. A corrected quantity needs an explicit order change and a fresh stock check.
